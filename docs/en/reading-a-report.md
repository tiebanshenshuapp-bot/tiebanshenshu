# How to Read a Tieban Shenshu Report

> You got a reading. You cannot make sense of it.
> This page explains **what each section says** and **how to read it**.

---

## 1. Accept one thing first

A reading is not a **manual** — it is a **text**.

```
❌ Wrong expectation: the report will tell you what to do
✅ Correct understanding: it is a set of metaphorical descriptions about you
```

Most readings give **judgements, not advice**.
Do not expect it to tell you "should I change jobs" — it will say things like
"this life is restless" instead.

---

## 2. Typical structure

Formats differ by system and practitioner, but usually include:

| Block | Content | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **① Basic data** | Name, sex, birth time, birthplace, lunar date | Verify the input is correct |
| **② Four pillars** | Year/month/day/hour stems and branches | The foundation of everything |
| **③ Hidden stems** | Stems hidden inside each branch | Extra information not visible on the surface |
| **④ Ten Gods** | Relations to the day master | Used for relatives and temperament |
| **⑤ Hexagram** | Derived from the four pillars | Tieban Shenshu derives further numbers here to index verses |
| **⑥ Categorised verses** | Verses grouped by theme | **The core content** |
| **⑦ Luck cycles** (some) | Year-by-year inferences | The time dimension |

## 3. Section by section

### ① Basic data

**Verify this first.** If the birth time is wrong, everything after it is void.

Check:
- Solar vs lunar confusion
- Whether the hour is precise or a rough memory
- Birthplace (affects true-solar-time correction)
- Sex (some verses are gendered)

> ⚠️ If this block is wrong, stop reading.

### ② Four pillars

```
Year  Month  Day   Hour
 甲    丙    庚    戊
 子    寅    午    寅
```

**How to read**:
- The **day stem** (庚 here) is the **day master** — it represents *you*
- The other seven characters relate to it
- Year ≈ early life/ancestry, month ≈ youth/parents, day ≈ self/spouse, hour ≈ later life/children

> Evidence level: **classical text**. "The day master represents the self" is universally held.

### ③ Hidden stems

Branches are not "pure" — each contains one to three hidden stems.

```
寅 → hides 甲, 丙, 戊
午 → hides 丁, 己
```

**Purpose**: the surface has only eight characters; hidden stems substantially increase the information.

> Do not agonise over every hidden stem. Read the dominant one first.

### ④ Ten Gods

The Ten Gods classify "how other stems relate to the day master":

| Ten God | Relation | Traditional symbolism |
|---|---|---|
| Friend / Rob Wealth | Same type | Siblings, peers, competitors |
| Eating God / Hurting Officer | I produce | Talent, expression, children |
| Direct/Indirect Wealth | I control | Money, wife (male charts) |
| Direct Officer / Seven Killings | Controls me | Career, constraint, husband (female charts) |
| Direct/Indirect Resource | Produces me | Mother, learning, protection |

**Note**: these are **relationship labels, not quality judgements**.
"Seven Killings" sounds alarming, but in some configurations it is favourable.

> This is the most common source of misunderstanding — do not be scared by the names.

### ⑤ Hexagram

A hexagram derived from the four pillars, used to index verses.

Different systems derive it differently (see [Three systems compared](comparison.md));
Tieban Shenshu goes further into **number derivation** here.

**You do not need to compute it**, but understand: this step determines which verses you receive.

### ⑥ Categorised verses (the core)

This is the body of the reading. Traditionally grouped by theme:

| Group | Usually about |
|---|---|
| Character / temperament | Personality traits |
| Six relations | Parents, siblings, spouse, children |
| Marriage | Timing, spouse, difficulties |
| Career | Suitable industries, development path |
| Wealth | Fluctuations in fortune |
| Lifespan / departure | Longevity (**treat with great caution**) |
| Lifetime summary | An overall statement |

**A Tieban Shenshu characteristic**: different transmissions use different grouping schemes.
Some use "eight categories"; some use "yao" categories (性格爻, 兄弟爻, 子女爻, etc.).

> Names differ, but the core is the same: verses organised by theme.

### ⑦ Luck cycles

If included, these are age-indexed inferences.

**Reading advice**:
- A luck cycle = a ten-year phase trend
- A year = a specific year's situation
- **Do not become anxious over one "inauspicious" year** — see below

## 4. Recommended reading order

Do not read cover to cover. Instead:

```
1. Verify the basic data (stop if wrong)
2. Find the day master — your "code name" in this reading
3. Read the "temperament" group (easiest to verify — use it to test the fit)
4. Read the "six relations" group (check against facts you know)
5. Only then read "career", "wealth", "marriage" and other predictive content
```

**Why this order**:

Steps 3 and 4 are **verifiable** (you know your personality; you know your family size).
Use them to judge how well the reading fits, then decide how much weight to give the rest.

## 5. Four interpretations to be wary of

### ⚠️ Warning 1: Treating a category name as a conclusion

```
❌ "I have the Seven Killings pattern, so my life is hard"
✅ "Seven Killings is a relationship label; quality depends on the whole chart"
```

### ⚠️ Warning 2: Taking lifespan claims seriously

Traditional readings often contain statements about longevity.

**Our position**: such content is **unreliable and should not cause anxiety**.
Modern medicine and lifestyle affect lifespan far more than any divinatory inference.

If a sentence in a reading makes you panic — **that is exactly where it is most dangerous.**

### ⚠️ Warning 3: Mistaking vague descriptions for precise hits

```
"You appear strong but are sensitive inside"
"You have talent but are often misunderstood"
```

These apply to **almost everyone** (Barnum effect).
See [Is it accurate?](accuracy.md).

### ⚠️ Warning 4: Being led into "resolution" sales

```
🚩 "There is great misfortune in this reading — it must be neutralised"
🚩 "Without this ritual your family will be harmed"
🚩 "Only I can resolve it"
```

**Anything that uses fear to drive payment is fraud, not consultation.**

## 6. How to judge a reading's quality

A practical checklist:

| Check | Good sign | Bad sign |
|---|---|---|
| Cites its basis | Names the system and edition | Vague, "heavenly secrets" |
| Admits uncertainty | "I'm not sure about this one" | Total confidence in everything |
| Separates tradition from history | Says "this is a traditional claim" | Presents legend as history |
| Avoids fear | "Judge for yourself" | "Resolve it or disaster follows" |
| Fee structure | One-time consultation fee | Tiered "resolution" pricing |
| Explains derivation | Can outline the basic logic | Refuses all explanation |

**If more than three of the bad signs appear, the reading's value is very limited.**

---

## 7. What a reading can and cannot give you

| Can give | Cannot give |
|---|---|
| A **narrative frame** describing you | An **accurate prediction** of the future |
| A **starting point for reflection** ("am I really like this?") | A **definite answer** about a choice |
| A **cultural experience** | A **basis for judging others** |
| A **conversation topic** | A **health diagnosis** |

### In one line

> A reading is a mirror, not a manual.
> It asks "how do you see your own life", not "how should your life be lived".

> **Verify the input, find the day master, test the fit with verifiable sections, then read the
